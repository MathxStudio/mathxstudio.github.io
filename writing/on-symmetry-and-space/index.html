On Symmetry, Proportion, and Spatial Order

Proportion as Invariance

In both classical architecture and pure mathematics, beauty often emerges as invariance under transformation. Consider the golden ratio φ=1+52\varphi = \frac{1 + \sqrt{5}}{2}φ=21+5​​, which satisfies the self-similar relation:

φ2=φ+1\varphi^2 = \varphi + 1φ2=φ+1

When a facade or a grid respects a recurring modular ratio rrr, changing scale preserves the underlying spatial rhythm.
