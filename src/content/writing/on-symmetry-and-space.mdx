## Proportion as Invariance

In both classical architecture and pure mathematics, beauty often emerges as invariance under transformation. Consider the golden ratio $\varphi = \frac{1 + \sqrt{5}}{2}$, which satisfies the self-similar relation:

$$
\varphi^2 = \varphi + 1
$$

When a facade or a grid respects a recurring modular ratio $r$, changing scale preserves the underlying spatial rhythm.

### Minimal Constraints

Just as a well-posed theorem strips away redundant hypotheses, clean spatial design removes unnecessary ornament so that proportion, light, and material remain legible.
